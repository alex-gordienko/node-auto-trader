# Best prediction window with at most 3 entries a day — BTC / ETH (Binance spot)

Generated by `study.py` / `ob.py` / `report.py` on 2026-10-25.

## Setup

- **Data:** Binance spot 1-minute klines 2020-01 … 2026-10-23, with taker buy volume. Daily context comes from the same data plus Binance funding (3-day mean). The other coin's returns are included.
- **Features (30, all causal):** returns over 1 m … 3 d; realized vol 1 h / 1 d; volume ratios; taker imbalance 5 m / 1 h / 4 h; 1 h and 5 m range; distance from the 24 h VWAP, high and low; hour and weekday; trend vote 10–120 d; 30 d return; funding.
- **Decisions** every 5 minutes. **Horizons:** 5 m, 15 m, 1 h, 4 h, 12 h, 1 d, 3 d.
- **Models:** gradient-boosted trees (sklearn HistGradientBoosting) and ridge. Walk-forward: each test year 2022 … 2026 is predicted by a model trained on all data before it, with labels ending before the year starts.
- **Selection — the 3-a-day rule:**
  - Trade when the score is beyond the quantile 1 − 3/288 of the previous 30 days' scores (a causal threshold).
  - At most 3 entries per UTC day, one position at a time, held exactly the horizon.
  - `long` = spot long/flat. `ls` = long or short (needs perp or margin).
- **Costs** are per round trip and include 0.02 % slippage per side:
  - maker 0.02 % → 8 bps; maker 0.05 % → 14 bps; taker 0.1 % → 24 bps.
  - The bot's current 0.7 % per swap → 140 bps.
- **Controls:**
  - `random`: 3 random entries a day, same holding rules. It shows the market drift.
  - `oracle`: perfect foresight, the 3 best non-overlapping entries of each day. It is the upper bound before costs.
- Test period 2022-01-01 → 2026-10-23 (≈4.8 years). Sharpe is computed on daily PnL × √365. t = t-stat of the mean net return per trade (trades do not overlap).
